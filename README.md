## Description of the project
Electronics and programming project  
It is a raspberry pi pico 2 based tamagotchi style mascot keychain designed for students. It is meant to help you recording lectures, staying in the loop with google calendar integration and pomodoro timer to study without having to look at your phone's timer!
### What is "Wonder"?
Wonder is the name of the mascot! It is also the name of the app, and the physical device.  
### Why "Wonder"?
> "Wisdom begins in wonder." — Socrates
###  What does it do?
Currently it has three functionalities:
- recording lectures that are transcribed and polished by a local OpenSource AI and sent to your phone
- it has a Pomodoro timer for phoneless study sessions
- it can notify you of your classes if you setup your schedule in google calendar, anything else you got in there can also be notified by the physical device

### Components
